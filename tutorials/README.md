## What changed from the inference-only tutorial

The previous notebook (`TASK-INFERENCE`) ranked five drawn cartoon clips against their five names and recorded `top1_accuracy` 0.2 at chance as a finding. This notebook keeps those clips and their inference contract as Section 5 and adds the adaptation contract for a closed label set around them: a real labelled clip set fetched with a row-group pin and decoded with PyAV, a corpus-level protocol in the carried `metrics.py` (top-1 and top-3 accuracy, macro recall and F1, per-label confusion) with two non-adapted baselines, a bounded fine-tuning of the fusion head with the closed-set cross-entropy on cached tower features, clip panels, the drawn clips re-ranked after the shared head was tuned, and an artifact that reloads. The per-request `evaluation_report` helper is unchanged; `pipe.evaluate` is the corpus-level stage (`measured` / `measured-small-sample`). The package now batches clips through the vision tower (`classify_batch`, `EVAL_BATCH_SIZE` clips per forward), decodes video containers (`decode_clip`, PyAV — a new pinned dependency, `av==18.1.0`, BSD-3), and accepts zip/directory BYOD with a `labels.csv`.

## Conformance notes

- **Standalone carrier (ST1–ST8):** the primary path performs no clone, repository install, or repository import. Section 2 embeds the repository's three modules verbatim in topological dependency order (each tagged `metadata.dimer.embedded_module`; the only rewrites make `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS_DIR` working-directory-relative and remove internal relative package imports), Section 3 carries the 9-file snapshot manifest inline and asserts it against the module's constants before fetching, and `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE` records the repository, revision and module digests in the exports.
- **Parity (PAR1–PAR4):** `tests/test_notebook_parity.py` and `tools/validate_release_assets.py` fail when the carried modules, the inline manifest, or the inline pins differ from the repository, or when the notebook is not byte-identical to the generator's output.
- **Model acquisition (MOD1–MOD9, MOD12):** `stage_missing_files(WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` fetches only the manifest entries a fresh runtime lacks, at the pinned revision (never `main`); `verify_snapshot` re-hashes every entry and asserts the pinned `model.safetensors` digest and byte count before the processor or the model is constructed; `XClipVideoClassificationPipeline.from_pretrained(weights_dir=WEIGHTS_DIR)` loads the verified files through `XCLIPModel` / `XCLIPProcessor` (`local_files_only=True`, `trust_remote_code=False`) in float32 and picks CUDA when it is visible. No cell outside the carried modules calls `huggingface_hub`, `transformers`, `safetensors`, `pyarrow`, `av`, `urllib`, `torch.optim` or the model's processor directly.
- **Data (DAT1–DAT9, ST6):** the default sample is HMDB51 (`mteb/HMDB51` parquet repack at the immutable conversion revision `50bb2abb741074ef0868e59ba51af710c825cd1f`; the dataset is CC BY 4.0, Serre Lab): row group 0 of `default/test/0000.parquet` read with an HTTPS range request in the carried `samples.py` (the shard's declared size and row count checked first, the row group refused unless its SHA-256 and byte total match `ROW_GROUP_PINS`; about 113 MB), and `read_corpus` makes each row of the ten sample classes a record with its 8 uniformly spaced frames, its natural-language label and its source video; `build_sample_dataset(seed=SPLIT_SEED)` draws a source-grouped 181 / 53 / 66 and `check_split_disjoint` asserts no clip (by decoded-pixel digest) and no source is shared. The template adds a second *External access* bullet naming the host, the revision, the byte count and the licence.
- **Stages (§4.1):** `fetch_corpus` + `read_corpus` + `build_sample_dataset` / `load_byod_dataset` + `split_dataset` (sample acquisition) → `validate_dataset` per split + `check_split_disjoint` + refusal probes (validation, with three digests) → `validate_inputs` with the three-frame refusal probe + `classify` for five drawn clips with structural checks + `evaluation_report` against the drawn intentions (inference contract) → `chance_baseline` + `majority_baseline` + `pipe.evaluate` on the frozen model with four rankings printed (baselines) → `pipe.adapt` (bounded fine-tuning) → `pipe.evaluate` on validation and test with the comparison (evaluation) → the clip panels + the drawn clips again (inference) → `pipe.save_artifact` → `XClipVideoClassificationPipeline.from_artifact` with the parity assertion (export and reload).
- **Score semantics (UNC1–UNC4):** the probabilities are a softmax over the caller's label set — a ranking, not a calibrated probability — and the model never abstains; the notebook says so before the first ranking; the corpus rates are top-1 / top-3 accuracy, macro recall and macro F1 over one reference label per clip with the per-label confusion beside them; no acceptance threshold ships. The drawn clips re-ranked after adaptation carry a `sample-sanity` verdict against intentions drawn in code, and the notebook names the missing dispersion.
- **Baselines and evidence (EVAL6/EVAL10/EVAL11):** the chance baseline, the majority-label baseline and the frozen model are scored on the same 66 clips as the adapted model, on the same rates; every metric is labelled as one seeded split of one sample with no dispersion estimate, and the notebook says explicitly where the frozen model sits (build record: the zero-shot prompts already rank 53 of the 66 clips first; the misses sit on `chewing` (3 of 7, the rest called `drawing a sword` or `brushing hair`) and `clapping hands` (3 of 6, called `brushing hair` or `doing a cartwheel`) — close-up upper-body actions the prompts do not separate) and that the gain is measured on ten HMDB51 actions only.
- **Split integrity (SPL1–SPL8):** the split is seeded, clip-disjoint and source-disjoint; validation (53 clips) selects the epoch on top-1 accuracy and the test split is used for nothing but the final evaluation; the notebook states that clips cut from one source video share its scene and actor and must be split by source, camera or session.
- **Ceilings (VAL6):** `NUM_FRAMES` (8), `FRAME_SIZE` (224), `MIN_IMAGE_SIDE` (16), `MAX_IMAGE_SIDE` (4096), `MIN_LABELS` (2), `MAX_LABELS` (32), `MAX_LABEL_CHARS` (64), `MAX_TEXT_TOKENS` (77), the record bounds (16..5,000), `MIN_PER_CLASS` (2), `EVAL_BATCH_SIZE` (8) and the id pattern are stated in the Prerequisites and printed before the model runs; four dataset refusals and one clip refusal are demonstrated.
- **BYOD (DAT10–DAT19):** `USE_BYOD` defaults to `False` so the sample path never opens an upload dialog; when enabled, one zip is read member by member (names flattened, no `extractall`) or a directory walked, each row of `labels.csv` becomes a record (the clip decoded by PyAV, or by Pillow for GIF/WebP), and the records flow through the same validation, source-disjoint split, baselines, fine-tuning, evaluation, export and reload cells. The expected layout and the privacy guidance are stated in the Prerequisites and in Section 4, and the training split's summary is written to `outputs/xclip_video_classification_train.csv`.
- Licence: the weights are MIT and the notebook, README, card and `docs/WEIGHTS.md` say so; HMDB51 is CC BY 4.0 (attributed to Serre Lab, Brown University; Kuehne et al. 2011) and is not redistributed — the export records the corpus name, repository, revision, file, licence, row groups and classes under `corpus`.
- `tools/validate_release_assets.py` performs source validation only. It does not satisfy the clean-runtime execution requirement; a release review must confirm that a recorded clean run in `../docs/release-verification.md` matches the notebook revision under review before the status is promoted to `Release-grade`.

- `xclip_video_classification_evaluation_report.json` — baselines, frozen and adapted metrics on validation and test, the per-clip rows with both rankings, the comparison, the adaptation history
- `xclip_video_classification_examples/` — six held-out clips as panels (the 8-frame strip with the reference label, the frozen top-3 and the adapted top-3)
- `xclip_video_classification_adapter/` — `adapter.safetensors` + `manifest.json`
- `xclip_video_classification_result.json` — `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE`, model identity, snapshot, corpus, the drawn clips before and after adaptation with their reports, comparison, artifact digest, reload parity, runtime
